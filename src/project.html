This page holds the architecture, literature survey, plan and team for reviewers.

01Architecture

Local where it's cheap. Cloud where it persists.

The deployed app is serverless on AWS. Videos, transcripts, notes and pending references live in the user's own library in the cloud, so everything is there on the next sign-in from any device. Project Review-1 proposed Supabase, a local Whisper worker and Gemini; the deployed version uses the AWS equivalents. Pick a flow and follow the data.

      02Literature survey
      
    Standing on fifteen papers.
    
      
    Transcription, summarization and retrieval are well studied. We reviewed work from 2020 to 2024 and built the extension on top of it. Filter by theme, search, or open any paper by DOI.
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Research gap: what we borrow and what we extend
    
      
    The reviewed papers support transcription, summarization and retrieval. The extension connects explicit references to supporting video sections.
    
      
    
      
    This describes the project's integration scope. It is not a claim that no existing product supports similar functions.
