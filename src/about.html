01The problem

Long videos hold knowledge you can't easily reach.

Online learning, meetings, training sessions and tutorials produce hours of recordings. Watching everything again to find one explanation, or to check your own notes, is slow and repetitive.

02The platform

From raw recording to searchable knowledge.

Upload lecture videos, meeting recordings, training sessions or video links. The platform transcribes the speech, organizes it into notes and lets you ask questions in plain language, then jumps to the exact moment in the video.

Speech to transcript

Speech-to-text produces a timestamped transcript, saved in small chunks so every claim can point back to a moment.

Notes and summaries

Organized notes, summaries and key points, each linked to its source section of the video.

Important timestamps

Jump straight to the section that matters. No scrubbing through an hour to find one step.

Action items and decisions

For meetings and training sessions, surface what was decided and what needs doing.

Semantic search

Search by meaning or ask a natural-language question. Matches come back with video timestamps.

Cloud-backed

Scalable storage, remote access from authorized devices and centralized data for large video files.

03Standout features

The part that connects your whole library.

Transcripts and search are table stakes. These features connect your recordings to each other, with the evidence on screen and you always in control.

Video Context Recovery

Detects phrases such as "as discussed earlier" or "refer to the previous video", uses the surrounding sentences to search your other videos, and shows up to three matching excerpts with titles and timestamps.

My Notes vs Video

Paste your notes, pick a video. See covered points, missed key points and possible mismatches, each with a source timestamp. Nothing changes until you approve it.

Remember the Missing Explanation

No source yet? Save the unresolved reference. After each new upload is indexed, a worker checks it and sends an in-app notification, once, when a candidate appears.

For teams and educators

Library Map: see what your recordings assume but never explain

Every confirmed reference becomes a link. Get a dependency map, the order to watch things in, and a gaps report for instructors and teams: the exact explanations nobody has recorded yet.

New

Follow-up Tracker: make sure every "next video" gets made

"We'll cover that in the next video." CloudNote AI catches the follow-up, watches every later upload, tells you once when it is covered, and lists the follow-ups no video has covered. In our search of existing note and meeting tools we found nothing that does this.
