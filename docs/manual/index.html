1. What CloudNote AI does

CloudNote AI turns recorded lectures, meetings and tutorials into notes you can search and question. Upload a video or audio file, or import a transcript, and CloudNote AI will:

  • Transcribe the recording with Amazon Transcribe into timestamped moments.
  • Write notes: a summary, key points, action items and decisions. Every item links to the moment it came from.
  • Answer questions from your notes, with numbered citations you can click.
  • Connect recordings: when one video says "as we discussed earlier", it suggests where another video explains it (Context Recovery).
  • Remember gaps: if nothing explains a reference yet, it can keep checking every new upload and notify you once when something matches.
  • Track follow-ups: phrases like "in the next video we will cover..." are tracked until a later video covers them.
  • Map your library: which videos depend on which, a suggested watch order and a gaps report.

Each user only ever sees their own library.

We found no existing tool that combines cross-video reference recovery with remembered, re-checked gaps in this way. That is our observation from the tools we reviewed, not a claim that none exists.

2. Getting started

Create an account

  1. Open https://d349qer3boe6fg.cloudfront.net.
  2. Click Create an account under the sign-in form.
  3. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters that includes a number, then submit. You are signed in straight away; there is no confirmation email.

Try it with sample videos

A new library is empty. Click Try with sample videos to add two sample videos (Server Setup and Application Setup Tutorial). The Library page offers the other two (Database Tutorial, Monitoring and Alerts). Samples cost nothing: they skip transcription.

3. Home and Library

Home greets you, offers a quick question box and shows your library at a glance: number of videos, hours indexed, searchable moments and open items. Needs your attention lists references that may now be answered and follow-ups still waiting.

Library shows every video as a card with cover art, status and length. Use the filters and search box to narrow the list, and the sample buttons to add the remaining sample videos.

4. Adding videos and transcripts

Open Add video. There are two ways to add a recording:

OptionWhat to provideWhat happens
Media filemp4, m4a, mp3, wav, flac, ogg, webm or amr, up to 500 MB. Choose the spoken language.The file uploads straight to private storage (Amazon S3), Amazon Transcribe converts the speech to text, and the notes are generated. A short clip takes about a minute.
TranscriptSRT, WebVTT, Amazon Transcribe JSON or plain text, pasted or typed.Notes are generated right away. No transcription cost.

Languages: English (US, India, UK), Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French and German. Each user can keep up to 30 videos.

5. The video page

Open any video from the Library. The left side has the player (or a note for imported transcripts), the Moments timeline and the full transcript with its own search box. Click any timestamp to jump the player to that moment.

The header shows the status, length, number of moments and how the notes were made: AI notes or extractive notes. The buttons Rename, Reprocess (regenerate notes) and Delete are at the top right.

The four tabs

NotesRead the summary, key points, action items and decisions. Copy as Markdown and Download .md export them.

5. The video page (continued)

6. Notes and ask-your-notes

The Notes page collects the notes of every ready video as compact cards. Filter them with the box at the top, or open a card to read it full page. The reader (#/notes/<video>) has its own search box for searching inside that note.

Ask your notes

Type a question in Ask your notes, or click a suggested question. The AI answers only from your notes and cites the passages it used with numbers like [1]. The sources are listed under the answer with links to their moments. When your notes do not cover the question, it says so and shows the closest notes instead of guessing.

6. Notes (continued) and 7. Ask

7. Ask: searching your library

The Ask page searches every transcript in your library and returns playable moments, best match first. It is the fastest way to find where something was said.

8. Context Recovery, remembered references and notifications

Recordings often lean on each other: "use the database settings from the earlier tutorial". The Context tab lists these references, each with its moment and the best candidate explanations from your other videos.

  • Accept this source links the reference to that explanation.
  • Leave unresolved and remember it keeps the reference as a saved question. CloudNote AI re-checks it every time you add a video.

Remember the Missing Explanation

When a new video explains a remembered reference, the bell shows a badge and the notification panel offers the match once. Accept it to create the link, or dismiss it.

9. Follow-ups and the Library map

A follow-up is a forward reference such as "in the next video we will explain indexes". CloudNote AI tracks each one until a later video covers it, and lists those still waiting on Home and in the gaps report.

The Library map draws your videos and the links between them: confirmed links, gaps (missing explanations and follow-ups not yet covered) and candidates to review. Below the map are a suggested watch order (sources before the videos that rely on them) and a gaps and follow-ups report you can copy.

10. Command bar and mobile

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere to open the command bar. Type to find a video or page, move with the arrow keys and press Enter. Esc closes it.

On a phone the side menu becomes a bottom tab bar (Home, Library, Notes, Ask, Map), and the bell and sign-out move to the top.

11. How it works

CloudNote AI is fully serverless on AWS: nothing runs while no one uses it.

PartAWS serviceRole
WebsiteS3 + CloudFrontServes the app over HTTPS; /api/* goes to the API.
Sign-inAmazon CognitoAccounts and tokens; the API rejects requests without a valid token.
APIAPI Gateway (HTTP API) + LambdaAll app requests.
DataDynamoDBVideos, transcripts, notes, references, notifications (one table).
MediaS3 (private)Uploads and playback through short-lived signed links.
Speech to textAmazon Transcribe + EventBridgeTranscribes uploads; EventBridge wakes the worker Lambda when a job finishes.
AI notes and answersAmazon Bedrock, with free fallbacksSee below.

Which AI writes the notes

The backend tries each model in order and uses the first one that answers:

  1. Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova Lite), the intended model.
  2. Groq free tier (openai/gpt-oss-120b).
  3. Google Gemini free tier (gemini-3.8-flash).
  4. Built-in extractive notes (key sentences from the transcript) if no model answers.
Current status (October 2026). The AWS account is new, and Bedrock allows it 0 tokens per day for now. Notes and answers on the live site therefore come from Groq, and library search is keyword based instead of meaning based. Bedrock takes over automatically when AWS raises the quota. Transcription always uses Amazon Transcribe.

12. Limits, privacy and troubleshooting

Limits

  • Up to 30 videos per user and 500 MB per file. One spoken language per upload.
  • No password reset yet, and sign-up does not verify email addresses.
  • Media links expire after a while; reload the page if playback stops.

Privacy

  • Your videos and notes are private to your account.
  • While Groq or Gemini writes the notes, transcript text is sent to that provider. Do not upload confidential recordings.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Sign-up is rejectedUse a password of 8+ characters with a number. If the site limits sign-up to certain email domains, use an allowed address.
Header says extractive notesNo AI model answered when the notes were made, for example because a provider was busy. Click Reprocess later.
Video stuck on transcribingShort clips finish in about a minute. After 30 minutes the video can be started again. Check that the file has speech in the chosen language.
"The upload did not finish"Upload the file again; keep the tab open until the upload completes.
Ask your notes finds nothingThe AI only answers from your notes. Rephrase with words used in the video, or check that the video's notes are ready.
Playback stopsThe signed media link expired. Reload the page.

Lessons from the first deploy

  • aws cloudformation package ignores Globals.CodeUri; each function needs its own CodeUri.
  • New AWS accounts on the Free plan cannot use Amazon Transcribe; the paid plan (which keeps remaining credits) is required.
  • Bedrock on a new account may have a 0 tokens per day quota; ask AWS Support for an increase, and use the free fallbacks meanwhile.
  • Model failures are logged in CloudWatch as model failed, trying the next one; replies the app cannot use are logged as ask: reply had no usable citations.

Security checklist

  • Set ALLOWED_EMAIL_DOMAINS to limit sign-up, and keep an AWS Budgets alert on the account.
  • Never commit AWS, Groq or Gemini keys. Delete the deploy access key when deployments are finished.
